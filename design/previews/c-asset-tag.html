Principles

  • An asset is a physical object, so its card is one too: brushed metal in light, anodised gunmetal in dark. This is the only skeuomorphic touch in the system.
  • Brass marks things of record: exporting, locked logs, the verified report. Teal is for everyday actions, in both themes.
  • Dark mode is a tonal version of the same palette, not an inversion. Surfaces carry a trace of teal, and status colours drop to pastels on dark containers.
  • A prediction is a window with a most-likely dot. The width of the window is the confidence.
