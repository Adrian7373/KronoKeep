Principles

  • Each schedule reads at a glance like a gauge: where the needle sits, and how wide the hatched band of doubt is.
  • Amber is the instrument colour (needle, odometer, focus ring) and never a status colour.
  • Numbers are condensed with tabular figures so km and days never jitter while they update.
  • Motion appears only when the user acts. The odometer rolls when updated, and nothing else moves by itself.
