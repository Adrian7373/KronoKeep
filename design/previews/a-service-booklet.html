Principles

  • History reads as a booklet: ruled lines, a stamp for each entry, one empty slot for the next service.
  • Red means only two things, overdue or sealed. It never decorates.
  • Numbers (km, ₱, days) use the display face with tabular figures, so they line up down the page.
  • A prediction always shows its range ("9–13 d", "± 2 days") and never a single fake-precise date.
